## 0. TL;DR: the one-paragraph plan

Build a **13-slide hybrid deck** for Civic (AI-native 311 replacement) that follows a **problem-first strategic-narrative arc** (Raskin: Shift → Enemy → Promised Land → Magic → Proof). Each slide = **one idea, ≤10 words of on-screen text, 40pt+ type, ~65% visual**. The single most persuasive asset is a **real screen recording of the working app**, Higgsfield supplies *hero shots, atmosphere, and b-roll only*, never fake UI, fake data, or fake team. Produce ~25-35 stills (Seedream 4.5 / Nano Banana) + 4-6 short video clips (Veo 3.1 Lite for b-roll, Seedance 2.0 for the 2 hero shots), upscale to 4K, assemble in a slide tool (Canva/Figma/Slides), then time it to ≤5 min. **Lock copy and the demo recording before generating any media.**

## 1. Goals, audience & constraints

### 1.1 What this deck must do
Civic's go-to-market is **resident-first / pressure-led**: win residents → make city performance publicly legible → sell the back-office integration once the city feels political pressure. The deck must land that story to a **mixed room** in one pass.

### 1.2 Mixed-audience weighting (what each viewer scores)
| Viewer | Weights most | We satisfy with |
|---|---|---|
| **Hackathon judges** | Working demo (30%), market/impact potential (40%), novelty (20%), social impact (10%) | Slide 5 live demo + S6 magic + S11 impact |
| **VC investors** | Market size, traction, business model, why-now, founder fit | S3 Why-Now, S7 bottom-up market, S8 model, S9 traction |
| **Gov / enterprise buyers** | Technical approach, outcome KPIs, trust, low-risk adoption (pilot) | S11 outcome metrics + trust-by-design, S9 pilot path |
| **Impact investors** | Theory of change, additionality, outcome data, mission durability | S2 problem ToC framing, S11 outcome KPIs |

**Design implication:** lead with **demo + felt problem** (wins judges and the room emotionally), then satisfy the analytical viewers with market/model/traction, then close on impact + vision. Do **not** dilute the front of the deck with TAM math, that's S7.

### 1.3 Hard constraints (from research, apply to every slide)
- **One idea per slide.** If you must say two things, use two slides.
- **≤10 words on screen** for a presented deck (YC Demo Day rule: 7 words; we allow up to ~10). Speaker narrates the rest.
- **Font ≥40pt** (titles 54-72pt; data labels 28-36pt floor). Readable on a phone.
- **~65% visual / 35% text.** Zero paragraphs. Ever.
- **Title = the "so what"**, not a label. ("Cities are flying blind" not "Problem").
- **One striking number per data slide.** Not a table dump.
- **≤5 min spoken** (mixed-room sweet spot; judges often cap at 3-5). Build a 3-min and 5-min cut.

## 2. Research synthesis: the rules this deck is built on

*(Full source list in §12. This section is the distilled, decision-ready version.)*

### 2.1 Narrative arc: strategic narrative (Andy Raskin) over feature-listing
The strongest investor decks tell a **change story**, not a product story:
1. **Name the Shift**: an undeniable macro change (here: vision-LLMs got cheap + accurate; smartphones universal; accessibility now legally mandated).
2. **Name the Enemy**: *not a competitor*: the old way. Here the enemy is **the 311 black hole**, reports vanish, no accountability, cities fly blind.
3. **Tease the Promised Land**: a city where you snap a photo and watch it get fixed, on a scoreboard the city can't hide.
4. **Introduce features as "magic gifts"**: only after the story: auto-work-order, public scoreboard, on-device privacy.
5. **Show proof**: built MVP, Open311-conformant, Cumming pilot, lead pipeline.

**The hero is the resident (Luke). Civic is the lightsaber. The founders are Obi-Wan.** Never make the product the hero.

Supporting arc (emotional ordering): **Hearts → Minds → Wallets.** Make them care, then make them understand, then make the ask.

### 2.2 Canonical frameworks (we synthesize, not copy)
- **Sequoia (10 sections):** Purpose → Problem → Solution → **Why Now** → Market → Competition → Business Model → Team → Financials → Vision. *Why-Now is first-class and sits before market. We keep that.*
- **YC Demo Day:** Problem (quantified) → Solution → Demo → Market (bottom-up) → Why-now → Traction → Model → Team → Ask. "Don't bury the lead, lead with traction if it's strong." 7 words/slide. 40-60pt font.
- **Kawasaki 10/20/30:** 10 slides, 20 min, 30pt min font. We honor the *spirit* (ruthless compression), not the literal count.
- **Airbnb (gold standard):** 10-14 slides, one claim per slide, market *validation* before market *size*, business model in one sentence, proof via a real event. **Eye-path control**, size the value props so the eye hits them in priority order.

### 2.3 Slide-level do/don't (the rules that kill decks if broken)
- **Problem:** Size + Severity in one line + one big number; name *who* suffers; make it visceral; **never** preview the solution; one problem, not three.
- **Solution:** the inverse of the problem in one sentence; customer *outcome* not tech mechanics; ≤3 feature-benefits; one functional visual.
- **Product:** show the **real product working** (screenshot/GIF/recording with callouts). **Never** wireframes, architecture diagrams, or **AI-generated filler UI**. Investors read that as low effort.
- **Why-Now:** convergence of 2-3 *external, timestamped* forces. "If it could've been written 5 years ago, it's wrong." No generic "AI is growing."
- **Market:** **bottom-up** (# customers × ACV), not "1% of a $XB market" (universal red flag). Top-down only as a ceiling, with a named source. Headline = "$Xbn market, $Ybn reachable now."
- **Competition:** **feature matrix**, not a 2×2 magic quadrant (mocked by VCs). Name **real** competitors (incl. "doing nothing"/legacy 311). Never "we have no competition." 5-10 rows, ≤6 words/cell.
- **Team:** one **unfair-advantage** line per founder (the specific reason *this* person beats *this* problem). Never "passionate about the space." Max 3-4 faces. **Use real photos. Do not generate fake founders.**
- **Ask/Close:** specific and milestone-tied. **End on vision, not financials**: leave the room inspired, not analytical.

### 2.4 Design & visual principles
- Slides are **visual aids, not documents.** Trim copy by half, then again.
- White space is a feature; crowded slides "feel anxious."
- One dominant visual per slide; data as a **growth story** (line/bar with CAGR), never concentric TAM circles or pie charts.
- **Consistency = credibility:** one palette, one type system, one motion language across all slides. Inconsistency reads as amateur.
- **Motion:** use video for the **emotional hook** and the **product moment** only. Everywhere else it distracts. Cap clips; keep motion subtle (slow push-in, parallax). Silent or light sound design. Never let video fight the speaker.

### 2.5 Civic-tech / social-impact specifics
- **Trust is a product feature, not marketing.** Surface: on-device privacy (face/plate blur), open standards (Open311), public dashboards, data governance ("who owns the data if the city leaves?").
- **Outcome metrics, not output metrics.** "Reduced median pothole resolution from 7 days to 2" >> "sent 12,000 notifications." Anchor 3-5 headline KPIs.
- **Theory of Change:** inputs → activities → outputs → outcomes → impact. At pre-seed, show ToC *intent*; don't fake results.
- **Pilot-first GTM:** cities can rarely sole-source a full contract but *can* fund a $25k, $150k pilot. Pitch the pilot, not the enterprise deal. Sales cycle 6-18 months, name it honestly; it's a moat (low churn once won).
- **Knight Foundation triangle:** Impact + Investment + Demand must all be present. Weakness in one collapses the others.

### 2.6 Practitioner doctrine (YC partners & top VCs): the lines that move the room
- **Clarity before persuasion (Dalton Caldwell, YC):** "If people can't follow you, there's no way you're getting them to yes." Buzzwords are disqualifying. The investor must leave **energized**, not merely informed. "a wishy-washy maybe is a no."
- **Make it sound dumber (Michael Seibel, YC):** "You don't need to sound cool, you need to be clear. Make it sound dumber than you think it should. 80% accurate + 100% understandable beats the reverse." Build a **30-sec** pitch (what you do · market · traction) and a **2-min** pitch (+ unique insight · model · team · ask). **Email test:** send your two-sentence "what we do" to a smart friend outside the space; if they have questions, rewrite.
- **Never say "both" (Garry Tan, YC):** indecision on a core strategic choice kills the pitch. Civic is a **resident-first accountability platform** that sells a back-office integration. Say it that decisively; don't hedge.
- **Hierarchy that earns the lean-in (Garry Tan):** "Show me a big unaddressed problem and I'm listening. Show me the solution you built and I lean in. Show me the team of true believers and I invest." → problem > solution > team > (not) credentials. **Earnestness is the #1 trait**. Sincerity reads on stage; hype doesn't.
- **Traction beats deck (Tan/Seibel):** "Actual demand is more persuasive than any deck." This is why **the live demo + Cumming pilot + 283 leads** sit early and carry weight. "I'm more interested in progress than genius ideas." Always attach **timeframes** to progress numbers.
- **Don't build a Frankenpitch (Tan):** *we just ran 20+ research streams*: synthesize into one voice; don't bolt every framework on. One spine (§3), one decision per slide.
- **Market framing (Gurley / Thiel / Andreessen):** start with **a big share of a small market, then expand** (Thiel), our Cumming→county→state beachhead is exactly this. Don't size Civic off the incumbent's tiny footprint ("sizing a disruptor off the incumbent is like counting cars by the horses in 1910", Gurley). "The market always wins" (Andreessen), so the expansion logic must be airtight.
- **⚠️ YC's "no video" caution vs. our hybrid format:** YC's *live Demo-Day stage* guidance says **no video, no cumulative graphs** (polish reads as suspicious in a 2-min projector pitch). Our deck is a **mixed-room hybrid**, where a hero clip + real screen-recording genuinely help, but honor the spirit: keep motion **subtle and strippable**, and maintain a **video-free "judge cut"** (static stills + the real demo recording) so the deck works in a bare-bones room. Never let a clip imply traction it doesn't have.

## 3. The Civic narrative spine (the through-line)

> **Old world:** You report a pothole into a 311 void. Nothing happens. No one is accountable. The city has no idea how it's doing. *(Enemy = the black hole.)*
>
> **The shift:** A phone in every pocket + a vision model that classifies, costs, and routes a repair for **$0.0001** + an April-2026 law that makes city services legally accountable for accessibility. The window just opened. *(Why now.)*
>
> **Promised land:** You snap a photo. In under 5 seconds it's a costed, routed work order on the city's desk, and a public scoreboard the city can't hide. *(Solution + magic.)*
>
> **Proof:** It's built. It's Open311-conformant. Cumming, GA is the beachhead. 283 named leads in the pipeline. *(Traction.)*
>
> **Vision:** The accountability layer for every piece of public infrastructure in America.

**One-liner (cover):** *"See it. Snap it. Your city fixes it. On a scoreboard they can't hide."*
**Declarative purpose (Sequoia's "one sentence"):** *"Civic is an AI-native replacement for the city 311 system that turns a resident's photo into a costed, routed, publicly-tracked repair."*

For each slide: **on-screen headline** (the "so what"), **content** (what the speaker proves, real data only), **visual concept**, and **Higgsfield approach** (model · aspect · motion). All slides **16:9, 1920×1080 master.**

> **Source-of-truth rule (non-negotiable):** UI = real screenshots/recording. Data = cited numbers from `docs/planning/context.md` & `leadgen/`. Team = real photos. Higgsfield = hero/atmosphere/b-roll only.

### S1: Cover
- **Headline:** `Civic` + *"See it. Snap it. Your city fixes it."*
- **Content:** logo/wordmark, one-liner, presenter/team, event. Nothing else.
- **Visual:** clean hero, a stylized city seen as a living map, brand color, deep negative space for the wordmark. Product-led, not stock-photo.
- **Higgsfield:** **Seedream 4.5**, 4K, 16:9 → animate to a **6-8s hero loop** via **Veo 3.1 Lite** (start_image = the still; motion = slow aerial push-in / subtle map-pin shimmer). This clip also opens the standalone sizzle.

### S2: Problem (the Enemy: the 311 black hole)
- **Headline:** *"Cities run on a broken 311."*
- **Content:** ONE big number + visceral line. Use real, cited stats: **NYC: 42M 311 contacts/year**; **Winnipeg: hazardous sidewalk repair averages 61.8 days vs a 2-day target**; Memphis potholes 3-7 business days. Name who suffers: residents shout into a void; staff drown in unstructured intake; leadership flies blind. *(One problem, not three, the void.)*
- **Visual:** a single cinematic-but-clean frame: a real-feeling broken street (pothole/dead streetlight) at golden hour, with the big stat as overlay typography. Emotional, not busy.
- **Higgsfield:** **Nano Banana** or **Seedream 4.5** still (photoreal street, generous sky for text). Optional 4s **Veo 3.1 Lite** slow dolly over the crack. **Data overlay is design, not generated.**

### S3: Why Now (the Shift)
- **Headline:** *"The window just opened."*
- **Content:** convergence of **external, timestamped** forces, (1) vision-LLM classification now **~$0.0001/photo** (Gemini 2.5 Flash); (2) smartphone ubiquity; (3) **WCAG AA became law for cities >50k as of April 2026**; (4) incumbents only *just* shipped photo categorization (**SeeClickFix, Jan 29 2026**) and stop at a suggestion. "None of this was true five years ago."
- **Visual:** 3-4 converging force-lines/icons meeting at a "now" point. Minimal, confident, data-forward (bold editorial moment inside the clean system).
- **Higgsfield:** mostly **design/data-viz** over a generated abstract gradient/mesh background (**Seedream 4.5**, 16:9). Low media spend here.

### S4: Solution (the Promised Land)
- **Headline:** *"Snap a photo. Watch it get fixed."*
- **Content:** the inverse of the problem, one sentence: *AI-native 311. A resident photo becomes a costed, routed work order in under 5 seconds, posted to a public scoreboard.* Frame as **resident outcome**, not tech. ≤3 magic-gift bullets (snap → auto work order → public accountability).
- **Visual:** the hero product moment, a hand holding a phone showing the **real app**. Generate the hand/phone/environment; **composite the real screenshot onto the screen** (Seedream image-editing can place it, or composite in the slide tool).
- **Higgsfield:** **Seedream 4.5** (hand+phone scene, 4K) → real UI screenshot composited. Optional **Seedance 2.0** hero clip (4K, identity-consistent) of the phone lifting into frame. This is one of only two "hero video" spends.

### S5: Product / How it works (THE DEMO)
- **Headline:** *"From photo to work order in 5 seconds."*
- **Content:** the 3-step pipeline shown **live or as a real screen recording**: (1) 2-tap photo submit (faces blurred on-device); (2) AI returns category + confidence + **costed work order** (dept, crew, materials, $cost, SLA badge); (3) pin drops live on the public map. **This is the most persuasive slide in the deck. It must be real.**
- **Visual:** real screen recording (15-30s) with callouts on the 1-2 "aha" moments. NOT a feature gallery.
- **Higgsfield:** **none for the UI.** Higgsfield only supplies an optional ambient background or a transition wipe. *Do not generate fake product screens.*

### S6: The Magic / Differentiator
- **Headline:** *"Not a suggestion. A work order."*
- **Content:** the unique claim. Incumbents stop at *category suggestion*; Civic generates the **full work order** (crew type, materials list, estimated minutes, dollar cost, SLA due date) **and** posts a **public accountability scoreboard** the city can't hide. Privacy-by-construction (on-device blur) as a trust gift.
- **Visual:** a clean before/after. "what they do" (a tag) vs "what we do" (a structured costed work order card). Use real work-order UI.
- **Higgsfield:** **none/minimal**: this is real-UI + design. Optional generated texture background only.

### S7: Market (bottom-up, beachhead-first)
- **Headline:** *"$1B+ market, starting in one county."*
- **Content:** **bottom-up** build, not "1% of X." Drivers: **~19,000 US municipalities + tens of thousands of HOAs**; Civic price band **$0.50, $2.00/capita/yr**. Worked example: **Cumming, GA city limits ≈ $4k, $16k; Forsyth County ≈ $125k, $500k.** Benchmark incumbent: **SeeClickFix/CivicPlus ≈ $57,750/yr for Bend, OR (pop 104k).** Show the beachhead (Cumming) → county → state expansion path.
- **Visual:** a **growth/expansion** visual (beachhead dot expanding across a US map) + a 2-row bottom-up table. **No concentric TAM circles.**
- **Higgsfield:** **Seedream 4.5** stylized US/Georgia map as background (4K, 16:9). Numbers + expansion arrows are design overlay.

### S8: Business model
- **Headline:** *"Free for residents. Cents per report."*
- **Content:** one-sentence model (Airbnb-style): *Residents free; classification costs ~$0.0001; cities pay for the back-office integration once residents create pressure.* **Land (residents) → expand (city contract).** Target all-in **<$0.001/report.** Note the GovTech economics: long sales cycle but very low churn once won.
- **Visual:** a simple land-and-expand flywheel (3 nodes). Clean diagram.
- **Higgsfield:** **none**: pure design. (Optional generated abstract bg.)

### S9: Traction & Go-to-Market
- **Headline:** *"Built, conformant, and in market."*
- **Content:** proof, not projections: **working MVP** (Next.js + Supabase, vision pipeline, staff inbox); **Open311 GeoReport v2 conformant** (interoperable with existing systems); **Cumming, GA beachhead confirmed** (not on SeeClickFix; incumbent Tyler/Connect2Forsyth is intake-only, no AI); **283 named leads** in `civic_outreach.csv` + ~528 structured leads across GA/TN/FL/NC/SC/TX/AZ/NV. Resident-first wedge = $0 CAC pressure engine.
- **Visual:** a milestone timeline + a "pipeline" counter (283) + the Cumming map pin. Real dashboard screenshot if available.
- **Higgsfield:** **minimal**: real screenshots + design. Optional generated Cumming/Georgia establishing shot (**Nano Banana**).

### S10: Competition (feature matrix)
- **Headline:** *"Everyone tags. We dispatch."*
- **Content:** **feature matrix**, Civic in the left column, real competitors across: **SeeClickFix/CivicPlus, Tyler Technologies, legacy 311 / "doing nothing."** Rows (customer-decision criteria, ≤6 words each): photo categorization · **auto-costed work order** · **public scoreboard** · on-device privacy · Open311 open API · price/capita · time-to-deploy. Acknowledge where incumbents are genuinely strong (installed base). Honesty builds trust.
- **Visual:** clean comparison grid; Civic column highlighted; ✓/✗ + a few hard numbers. **No magic quadrant.**
- **Higgsfield:** **none**: design only.

### S11: Impact & Accountability (civic-tech proof)
- **Headline:** *"Public infrastructure, finally legible."*
- **Content:** outcome-based KPIs (ToC framing): **median resolution time ↓**, **open-over-30-days count**, **by-neighborhood equity scoreboard** (does the nice neighborhood get fixed faster?), on-time SLA rate, vs neighboring cities. Trust-by-design: on-device blur, RLS default-deny, open data, public dashboards. **WCAG AA = legal requirement for cities >50k (April 2026)**. Civic ships it; incumbents don't.
- **Visual:** the equity scoreboard (real or realistic mock built from real schema). This is a high-wow, mission-defining slide.
- **Higgsfield:** **none for the chart** (real data viz). Optional warm generated "thriving neighborhood" establishing still (**Seedream 4.5**) as a section break.

### S12: Team
- **Headline:** *"Why us."*
- **Content:** ≤4 founders, **one unfair-advantage line each** mapping a specific credential to a hard problem Civic faces (build/ship evidence, civic/domain access, GTM). Active advisors only, one proof-line each. **[REQUIRES REAL INPUT, see §11 open decisions.]**
- **Visual:** real headshots + name + role + the one line. Clean, consistent crops.
- **Higgsfield:** **DO NOT generate faces.** Use real photos. Higgsfield only for a consistent background treatment / `remove_background` to standardize headshots.

### S13: Vision + Ask (close on inspiration)
- **Headline:** *"The accountability layer for public infrastructure."*
- **Content:** 5-year vision (every city's performance publicly legible, every report a tracked outcome) → then the **specific, audience-flexed ask**: judges → *"try the live demo"* (QR); cities → *"fund a 90-day pilot"*; investors → *"raising $X to reach Y cities in 18 months."* Investor ask = amount + **use of funds (3-4 categories, %)** + **18-24mo milestone it unlocks**; **no valuation on the slide**. Vision must be **10x, not 2x** (transformational) and logically reachable from today's wedge. End on vision, not numbers; never read a mission statement aloud.

- **Higgsfield:** **Seed300/Seedance 2.0 or Seedream 4.5 → Veo 3.1 Lite** hero loop (8s, slow rise over a thriving city). Second of the two hero-video spends. Reuse/echo the S1 hero for bookend symmetry.

**Optional compressions** (if cutting to 10-11 for a 3-min judge cut): merge **S5+S6** (demo carries the magic), merge **S8+S9** (model+traction), drop **S12** to a single line on the close.

## 5. Visual design system (clean modern / product-led)

> Lock this **before** generating media so every asset is consistent. Feed it into every Higgsfield prompt as a style suffix.

- **Palette (proposed):** deep civic **ink/navy** (`#0B1F3A`-ish) base · clean off-white canvas · **one confident signal accent** (a bright civic blue or teal) for "Civic action" · a restrained warning **amber/red** reserved *only* for the problem/SLA states. 3 colors max per slide.
- **Type (proposed):** a modern geometric/grotesque pairing, e.g., **Inter / Geist / Söhne**-style. Titles 54-72pt bold; body 28-40pt; data labels ≥28pt. High contrast, heavy weights (thin fonts vanish on projection/video).
- **Layout:** 12-col grid, generous margins, consistent title position, lots of white space. One dominant visual per slide.
- **Motion language:** subtle and uniform. Slow push-ins, parallax, soft pin-drops, gentle data reveals. No flashy transitions. Motion only where it earns attention (hook, demo, vision).
- **Imagery rule:** generated imagery = **hero/atmosphere/b-roll**. Real screenshots = product. Real photos = team. Real data = charts. This boundary is what keeps an AI-built deck from reading as "AI slop."
- **Logo:** if no Civic wordmark exists yet, design/generate options **first**: it anchors S1/S13 and the whole system. *(Open decision §11.)*

**Optional brand-consistency upgrade:** build a **Higgsfield Marketing Studio brand kit** (logo + palette + fonts) and use the **`ms_image`** model so every generated still inherits brand colors/tone automatically. Higher setup cost, maximum consistency. Decide in §11.

### 6.3 Prompt engineering approach
- **Every prompt** ends with the locked style suffix: *"clean modern product-led, [palette], generous negative space for typography, soft even lighting, photoreal, no text, no logos, 16:9."*

- **No baked-in text**: all words are added in the slide tool (generated text renders garbled and off-brand).
- **Lock a seed / style reference** across stills for visual consistency (or use the `ms_image` brand kit).
- **Video = image-to-video:** generate/approve the still first, then animate it (start_image) so motion inherits the approved look. Keep clips **4-8s**, subtle motion.
- **Aspect:** generate 16:9 master; `reframe`/`outpaint` for other ratios. Never upscale a low-res crop, regenerate.

- **Presented deck:** speaker narrates live; video clips are **silent loops** or have light ambient sound only (never compete with the speaker).
- **Standalone sizzle (optional 45-60s):** if a leave-behind / social asset is wanted, cut one from the same assets with **Seed Speech** VO (or human VO) + a **`generate_audio`** music bed. Script = the §3 spine. `reframe` it to 9:16 for social.
- **Recommendation:** human VO if available (more credible for a trust-driven civic product); TTS Seed Speech as the fast fallback.

## 8. Production workflow (order of operations)

1. **Lock copy first.** Write every slide's headline + speaker script. Time it. *(Media serves the message, never generate before copy is final.)*
2. **Record the real demo.** Screen-capture the working app (S5) + grab real screenshots (S4, S6, S9, S11). This is the credibility core. Get it before spending on generation.
3. **Lock the design system** (§5): palette, type, logo, grid, style suffix.
4. **Verify Higgsfield credits** (§6.5).
5. **Generate stills** (Seedream/Nano) → iterate against the style ref → `upscale_image` heroes to 4K → `outpaint`/`reframe` to 16:9.
6. **Composite real UI** onto generated device scenes.
7. **Generate the ≤6 video clips** (image-to-video from approved stills).
8. **(Optional) VO + music** for the sizzle.
9. **Assemble** in the slide tool (**Canva (MCP available), Figma, Pitch, or Google Slides**) add headlines, data viz, callouts, transitions. *Higgsfield is the asset factory; assembly happens here.*
10. **Review against the rubric** (§9), time the 3-min and 5-min cuts, cut ruthlessly.

**Assembly tool note:** the **Canva MCP** is connected. Designs, brand templates, and export can be driven from here when we move to build. Recommend Canva for speed + brand-template consistency, or Figma for pixel control.

## 9. Verification rubric (before "done")
- [ ] Every slide passes the **10-second scan test** (one idea, readable on a phone).
- [ ] **≤10 words** on screen per slide; titles are "so what," not labels.
- [ ] Font ≥40pt everywhere; ≥65% visual.
- [ ] **Zero fake assets:** no hallucinated UI, no fake metrics, no generated team faces. Every number traces to `context.md`/`leadgen/`.
- [ ] Problem lands emotionally **before** any solution appears.
- [ ] Why-Now uses external, timestamped forces.
- [ ] Market is **bottom-up**; competition is a **matrix** with real names incl. "doing nothing."
- [ ] Deck **ends on vision**, ask is specific and audience-flexed.
- [ ] Visual system is **consistent** across all 13 slides (palette/type/motion).
- [ ] 3-min and 5-min cuts both timed and rehearsed.

## 10. Risks & mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI imagery reads as generic "slop" | Strong locked brand system + seed/brand-kit consistency + real UI/data/team; restraint |
| Hallucinated UI/data erodes trust | Hard boundary: generated = atmosphere only; product = real recording; data = cited |
| Video distracts from speaker | ≤6 clips, subtle motion, silent loops in the live deck |
| Tone drifts slide-to-slide | One style suffix on every prompt; optional `ms_image` brand kit |
| Higgsfield credits insufficient | Verify first (§6.5); stills-only fallback path defined |
| Mixed audience dilutes message | Front-load demo+problem; satisfy analysts in the middle; flex only the Ask |
| Generated city scenes look fake/uncanny | Use real establishing shots where possible; keep generated to abstract/hero, not literal claims |
